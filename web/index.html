    Replace Polar keys

    Paste the Client ID and Client Secret from your client on Polar AccessLink. You sign in to Polar again afterwards; the synced history stays.

    Disconnect Polar?

    The app unlinks your account from your Polar client and deletes its synced copy. Health stops showing Polar data. Your data on Polar Flow is not touched.
